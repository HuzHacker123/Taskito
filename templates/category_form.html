About Categories

Categories help you organize your tasks into logical groups. Examples include:

  • Work - Tasks related to your job or professional projects
  • Personal - Tasks for your personal life and activities
  • Shopping - Shopping lists and items to purchase
  • Health - Exercise routines, medical appointments, etc.
  • Learning - Courses, books to read, skills to develop

You can create as many custom categories as you need to keep your tasks organized.
